## Order matching verification on 26 November 2026

The `dahinger-tracked-order-bill-results` build passed 561 tests, formatting, lint and the production build. Saving a billing start date in the sample dashboard opened the full order list and showed the verified Tally bill number and date. A seller could find, link and release a bill, and a voucher referencing a different order was blocked. Separate checks with two simulated sellers, Flipkart shipments and Tally sales vouchers verified billed and unbilled items in each of the five fulfilment types and a targeted Express retry. Starting bill matching alone left stock reservations inactive.

The initial walkthrough found the following five customer-visible gaps in that build.

- A Standard and a Fulfilment by Flipkart order item for the same seller, parent order and Tally stock item each claimed one voucher for two units. The full check put both in review. After the normal listing-mapping action, the stock check showed the Standard item as billed and reported 50 available out of 50 physical units with zero reserved. The post-mapping check needs the known competing bill claims before it can release a reservation. The warehouse item itself has no confirmed reservation rule.
- An invoice-only access failure discarded successful shipment search results for the affected type, even when the seller matched by order ID.
- An unmapped Smart order appeared in the tracked list while **Listings from orders** said no listings needed mapping. The tracked row offered no mapping action. Mapping the listing and confirming its **Set of** from the Tally item in **Inventory**, then reopening tracked orders, made **Find Tally bill** available with stock reservations still off.
- **Unresolved** filtered only the recent preview. The full tracked table still displayed billed rows, and an unresolved Smart order could be absent from the filtered preview.
- A second seller's failed check made the selected seller's order counts show **Unknown**, including types successfully read for the selected seller.

The revised dashboard opens one tracked list from the saved date and applies **All**, **Unresolved** and **Billed** to every fulfilment type in that period. In the built sample dashboard, saving 25 November showed two order items. **Billed** showed the order linked to `SAMPLE-INV-1` dated 26 November, and **Unresolved** showed the other order with **Find Tally bill**. In a separate screen test using the local service and database, a seller mapped an older Smart order from its row, confirmed its **Set of**, and reopened the order with bill lookup available and stock updates paused.

The stock check after mapping considers known competing bill claims from the other fulfilment types before releasing units. A regression test verified that adding a competing warehouse order invalidated a previously calculated availability and blocked release of the Standard order's reservation. All 565 tests, formatting, lint and the production build passed for that revision, which addressed the first, third and fourth findings above.

Follow-up checks covered the two remaining findings with simulated seller accounts and Tally sales bills. A Standard order returned by Flipkart remained visible and matched by Order ID. When the seller chose an invoice-number rule and Flipkart denied invoice access, the order still counted and its bill stayed under review until a retry verified the invoice number. In the dashboard test, a second seller's failure left the selected seller's eight Standard items visible and kept its other completed counts at zero. The warning identified the affected seller and provided a retry. A shared stock deduction still requires enough evidence to identify which sale it covers.

The follow-up revision passed all 573 automated tests, formatting, lint and the production build. Retrying the failed check recovered the invoice match across a restart, including when the same order had appeared under two fulfilment types.

A further review found cases where a missing or contradictory invoice could leave a sale marked billed or allow a stock increase. Coverage now includes a failed retry after an earlier successful shipment read, competing claims from another fulfilment programme or seller on the same ledger, and a cancellation reported after dispatch. The seller can still find a previously linked bill under **Unresolved** while the sale needs review. Short setup previews expire after ten minutes or at midnight in India.

In the built sample dashboard, saving a tracking date brought the order list into view. Searching for `SAMPLE-INV-1` found the automatically billed sale. Using that bill on the other order showed the conflicting reference and disabled confirmation. Switching to manual matching allowed linking and releasing the correct bill. At 320 and 390 pixels wide, the order and bill details remained readable, an order without an action used the full row, and closing the lookup returned keyboard focus to the original order. The walkthrough also verified saving a listing with its package count and stock updates paused.

The final revision passed all 580 automated tests, formatting, lint, the production build and all 24 extracted Windows pilot checks. In two browser tabs on the same sample company, a seller mapped a Smart sale dated 24 November from its order row. Changing the company start date from 23 to 25 November in the second tab and refreshing the first removed the older sale, updated both date displays and closed the obsolete date editor.

Comparison with Seller Hub and the customer's Tally books remains pending. Flipkart's current shipment schema lists the FBF profile, and its earlier seller guide excludes Flipkart Advantage orders from shipment search. Verify an enrolled seller's known warehouse order before treating an empty warehouse result as proof of zero sales. Confirm where the customer records transfers and sales in Tally before enabling any warehouse stock reservations. ## Current deliverable

The application can discover and read company-wide Tally stock, keep companies separate, display observations, prepare and retain local proposals, review their captured scope, preserve Pause and existing policies, save polling settings, record activity/diagnostics and create verified manual SQLite backups. A technician-assisted offline pilot restore validates backup identities/integrity, requires a preview digest, preserves current local settings and creates a safety copy before replacement. Restored observations require fresh reads, automation is disabled and listings are paused. Tally transport remains read-only. Inventory can publish a reviewed proposal, synchronize eligible listings after a Tally stock change, and lower shared listings when Stockbridge verifies a Flipkart sale. Customer acceptance should compare a single-tyre sale with the pair listing before billing and verify seller-reviewed recovery on a specifically authorized test destination. The seller chooses a package-based or manual Set of for each destination and explicitly confirms the company balance supplying it. [Tally adapter](../src/adapters/tally.ts), [inventory service](../src/server/service.ts), [HTTP routes](../src/server/http.ts), [backup manifest](../src/server/backups.ts), [pilot restore](../src/server/restore.ts)

The [tests](../tests/) exercise these behaviors through local HTTP responses, isolated databases and browser simulations. The pure [allowance helper](../src/domain/allowance.ts) is tested accounting arithmetic; it is not a delivered order importer or durable allowance-activation workflow. An Inventory draft does not authorize or change marketplace stock. In the separate Marketplaces view, live Apply sends a real edit and requires a fresh readback before reporting Updated.

The new Marketplaces view implements multiple explicitly added accounts, separate Windows-protected credentials, complete listing imports, seller binding and stock/MRP/selling-price Apply with durable attempts and readback. New live workspaces start with Add account; legacy saved primary credentials are adopted in place. Complete import must verify seller identity after restart or credential operations before Apply or Check result. A changed seller or duplicate seller account is rejected; an empty account cannot establish a new identity. Sample mode never contacts Flipkart. [Account service](../src/server/marketplace-service.ts), [publication](../src/server/marketplace-publication.ts), [adapter](../src/adapters/flipkart-listings.ts)

Inventory includes a company-wide activation preview and an optional, explicitly approved trial on one listing location. One unavailable company or an invalid pack on a selected listing can be isolated while healthy companies and valid drafts continue. Live customer verification of the trial and multi-company behavior remains open.

A manual zero pauses that listing and location. Finite sales allowances remain effective. Positive live stock under Limited sales is blocked until order reconciliation exists or an explicit Auto policy change removes that destination's constraint. Pause remains separate. Incomplete direct attempts become uncertain after restart without replay. Schema 4 persists mapping choices, package evidence and pending Tally changes. Earlier supported schemas are backed up before migration. Offline restore retains seller bindings, clears freshness and marks unfinished attempts uncertain; this does not implement production order reconciliation.
