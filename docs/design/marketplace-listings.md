# Marketplace account and listing management

**Status: Approved for implementation on 2026-11-18; account/listing management and direct Apply are implemented with local fixtures. Live account/API acceptance remains unverified.** The seller confirmed the consolidated flow and requested implementation. See [current delivery status](../../README.md) and the [inventory architecture](architecture.md).

## Confirmed scope

- Add an account-tile entry point for connected marketplaces. The first version must support multiple Flipkart seller accounts, with independent credentials, connection status and dashboards. Future marketplaces should fit the same navigation pattern.
- Opening a tile shows that seller account's marketplace listings, including listings without a Tally mapping. Listings come from Flipkart, not from the selected Tally company's stock items.
- This is a separate marketplace-management view. Selecting or connecting a Tally company is not a prerequisite. Existing Inventory remains the company-scoped Tally synchronization workspace.
- Use the Seller Hub listing page as the interaction reference: product/title, SKU, MRP, price, stock, settlement where supported, search and filters. The seller wants real marketplace edits, not only local stock proposals.
- The first version includes stock, MRP and seller selling-price editing. The seller accepted an Open in Flipkart action for settlement until a supported API is verified. Creating products, order management and full Seller Hub feature parity have not been requested for this flow.
- Each stock or price editor has its own Apply action, following Seller Hub. There is no required global Review changes -> Publish step for this view.
- Applying a stock edit in Marketplaces pauses automatic stock updates for the exact Tally-mapped listing and seller location. Show the lock and **Resume sync** on the destination.
- An existing sales allowance still limits direct stock edits. If the quantity conflicts with the allowance, require an explicit policy change before applying it.

## Implemented view

Navigation: **Marketplaces -> seller-account tile -> Listings**. A new live workspace shows Add account without prefilled seller tiles. Add account offers the marketplace picker, currently Flipkart only; each added account has independent credentials and status. Saved legacy primary credentials are adopted in place. Keep the account name and marketplace visible in the listing page header, with a route back to the account tiles and an Add account action at the entry point.

Fresh live storage has no seeded seller account or Tally company. Inventory's selector omits older unused placeholder seller records without changing their stored names. Explicitly added accounts and legacy accounts with listing records remain visible; saved legacy credentials are adopted before the initial dashboard snapshot. Company names come from Tally discovery. Returning from Marketplaces to Inventory refreshes the saved workspace snapshot immediately.

In Inventory, **Map listing** searches the selected seller account’s imported, unmapped listing locations by seller SKU, available title, FSN or Flipkart listing ID. Exact identifiers take priority over partial matches. Similar names or models can surface candidates when the seller opens the picker. Each location remains a separate choice, including when several warehouses share the same SKU or several offers share an FSN. The seller chooses the destination, confirms how many Tally units make up its **Set of**, confirms the company stock source and saves the mapping.

In **Order matching**, an identified tracked order already supplies the seller, listing and pickup. The seller can map the item and **Set of** from that row for bill matching. A billing-only mapping keeps the destination paused and leaves the stock source unconfirmed. The seller can confirm a stock source in Inventory when that location is ready for stock tracking.

Use one parent row per listing: image/title, seller SKU and product reference, status, MRP, seller selling price, observed stock and actions. Show the last successful refresh time and connection errors. Saved listings remain readable while account ownership needs verification. Refresh the account to enable stock and price edits and marketplace result checks. Title and SKU remain display-only unless a supported edit operation is verified.

Stock is location-specific. Expand a listing into location rows when it has several destinations; use the sole destination directly when it has only one. Do not interpret an edited total as an instruction to copy that quantity to every location. Price and MRP follow their API scope rather than being repeated as independent warehouse prices.

Refresh reads all five documented states and complete pagination; search and the status filter then cover the complete saved import. Account-wide results must cover all applicable listing states, not just Active. Additional filters such as brand, pack size or stock ranges require reliable fields and complete coverage of the requested result set; filtering one loaded page must not be presented as searching the entire account.

The listing source is Flipkart through the local backend. Local observations support display and filters, with timestamps and refresh state; this does not make Tally the source of listing data. Failed or partial refreshes must not remove unseen listings or imply a complete account import.

A recreated SKU can be imported with fresh, unmapped Inventory observations only when its previous identity has no mapping, allowance, pause or unconfirmed update. Protected identities still block the complete import until resolved. Completed update history remains attached to the original listing; old editors cannot write to the replacement.

## Per-field Apply interaction

The seller selected field-level Apply rather than a global review/publication step. The observed Seller Hub interaction is a pencil beside a row's value, a small editor and its own Apply button. The following details implement that choice:

1. Open the editor for the selected listing's stock or price value. Keep the current observed value visible and let the seller enter a new value; typing does not publish.
2. For Tally-mapped stock, tell the seller that applying the edit pauses automatic updates for that location. Display the existing policy and offer **Change stock policy** when the entered quantity conflicts with Limited sales. Revalidate the edit after the seller confirms the policy change. Only stock edits at a mapped location create a pause.
3. Apply validates and submits only that edit for the displayed account and destination. It must not submit other rows' drafts or unrelated price/stock edits. When the price API requires both MRP and selling price, preserve the unedited companion value from a fresh read; report a conflicting change rather than silently overwriting it.
4. Show Applying, Checking result, Updated or the specific failure beside the edit. Prevent duplicate submission while it is in progress. Preserve the entered value on failure so it can be corrected or retried. If a reply is lost, Reload saved result looks up that particular request for its seller account and listing. An uncertain result needs reconciliation before replay.
5. Closing or cancelling an editor before Apply discards its unsent edit. A later API result must stay bound to the original account/listing/location even if the user navigates to another account.

Apply is the explicit marketplace-write action for this view. It uses the shared backend publication and verification mechanisms; its familiar UI does not bypass validation or make an API request atomic across unrelated fields.

## Boundary with Tally synchronization

Separate pages can still refer to the same real marketplace destination. A direct stock edit therefore needs an explicit interaction with Tally automation. Merely hiding the Tally company selector does not isolate the stock being changed.

The direct-edit command means "set this account/listing/location stock to N". It does not automatically mean "allow N more sales", apply the tyre stock floor, or change physical Tally stock. Applying a manual zero pauses the mapped destination from either screen. See [manual zero and Tally sync](../adr/0007-manual-zero-pauses-sync.md).

For a mapped Tally item with a positive **Keep back from marketplaces** quantity, the stock editor checks how many complete packs remain after keep-back and unbilled orders. Entering more than that number requires confirmation of the exact quantity and pickup location. If available stock cannot be verified, any positive manual quantity requires confirmation. Apply rechecks the saved item, mapping and current availability before accepting an override. A manual zero is always within the keep-back limit. Recovery proposes a freshly calculated quantity under the normal stock policy.

The seller chose to preserve Limited sales constraints. If the remaining allowance is one, a direct quantity of five cannot bypass it. Show the current rule and an explicit Change stock policy action; never treat the entered stock as an implicit allowance grant. This applies even when automation is paused. Revalidate the current policy and allowance at dispatch as well as when opening the editor.

Once the validated Marketplace stock edit is recorded for submission, its mapped destination is paused. Rejected and uncertain submissions retain the pause. A seller can identify the location by its lock icon and **Tally sync paused** status in Inventory or Marketplaces. Flipkart sales can still reduce stock. Stockbridge continues observing stock and orders and skips automatic writes to that destination, including zero corrections. **Resume sync** unlocks the location and replaces its saved Inventory draft with the newly calculated quantity. If the calculation cannot be verified, Inventory shows the reason with an empty quantity. If company sync is enabled, a verified mapping is queued for a fresh calculation on the next eligible pass, even if the Tally balance has stayed the same. Any saved sales allowance still applies. When company sync is off, the destination waits for company enablement.

Price-only changes should leave stock automation unchanged. All stock writers must coordinate the same canonical destination and ordered publication path, so old Tally jobs cannot silently replay over a newer manual decision. Recheck destination, current versions and policy at dispatch. Track per-field/per-destination results: a price update may succeed while its stock update fails, and retry must not resend already confirmed changes unnecessarily.

## Implementation boundaries

- Account-scoped connection management uses separate protected credentials and seller bindings. Fresh import must verify the bound seller after restart or credential operations; different-seller replacement and duplicate sellers are rejected. Empty accounts cannot establish a new binding. Bind credentials and observations to verified seller identity; do not use a friendly tile name or SKU text as identity. Preserve the existing local credential protections described in [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-seller-owned-flipkart-self-access.md).
- Share account/listing/location identities between Inventory and Marketplaces. Add marketplace management commands separate from Tally-dependent calculation and proposal validation.
- Keep Flipkart transport, search pagination, field conversion, supported operations and per-SKU error handling in the Flipkart adapter. Other marketplace adapters can expose their supported fields without pretending that every platform has a Flipkart settlement control.
- Persist observations and direct-update attempts in schema 3; editor values remain local to the open editor until Apply. Writes use the account-serialized backend. Incomplete attempts become uncertain after restart and are not automatically replayed. Do not store account secrets in listing records or portable backups.
- Distinguish locally edited, submitted, confirmed and failed values. Readback/reconciliation confirms the edited value; the reviewed write documentation does not promise immediate propagation or an asynchronous job contract.

## Decisions from the follow-up interview rounds

- **Q4 revised on 2026-11-21.** The 18 November design offered **Update once** and **Pause Tally sync and update** in Marketplaces. The seller chose a single behavior so that a later Tally change cannot overwrite a manually entered marketplace quantity before the seller unlocks that destination. **Update once** remains available for separately reviewed Inventory proposals.
- **Q5 corrected by the seller:** use Seller Hub-style Apply for each stock or price editor. The earlier global Review changes -> Publish recommendation was not accepted for this view.
- **Q6 accepted:** start with supported stock, MRP and selling-price edits, with Open in Flipkart for settlement until a supported API is verified.
- **Q7 accepted:** require an explicit policy change before a direct stock edit can conflict with Limited sales. The 21 November decision replaced the separate manual zero hold with a destination pause.

## Delivery and remaining acceptance

The seller approved independent account tiles, account-wide listing views, supported fields, per-editor Apply, destination-specific automation pauses for direct stock edits, preserved policies and Seller Hub settlement fallback.

Direct Apply is available in live mode; sample Apply only changes sample observations. Mapped Inventory destinations also support reviewed publication and company-enabled automatic updates after Tally stock changes. A verified Flipkart sale can also reduce other eligible listings backed by the same item. Positive live stock under Limited sales is blocked because order reconciliation is unfinished. The separate Confirm automatic policy action removes a destination's allowance, retains Pause and sends no stock by itself.

Schema 10 retains the reason a resumed listing has no verified proposal. Schema 11 retains the catalogue identity for mappings with a pending or seller-selected pickup location. Schema 12 records a keep-back quantity per Tally stock item. Schema 13 persists the pending first proposal calculation for a listing mapped from tracked orders. Schema 14 records the billing tracking date and the observations used for all five fulfilment types. Schema 15 tracks keep-back quantities awaiting confirmation after a Tally base-unit change. Schema 16 also requires confirmation of positive keep-back quantities from older workspaces when the original unit was never recorded. Upgrades from schemas 1 through 15 create a verified backup. Items from schemas before 12 start with zero kept back. Offline restore preserves seller bindings, clears freshness and leaves unfinished attempts uncertain. Refresh the account before editing or checking outcomes. A seller can reconcile an uncertain mapped stock update once its previous request can no longer take effect. Restore retains current verified seller bindings and unfinished marketplace attempts even when newer than the selected backup. Current listing identities are retained as stale records, and restored versions advance beyond both saved and current versions so an old browser editor cannot become valid again. Conflicting seller identities, removal or weakening of current allowances, or loss of a paused destination refuse replacement and require reconciliation. A backup cannot erase evidence of a possibly sent marketplace request.
