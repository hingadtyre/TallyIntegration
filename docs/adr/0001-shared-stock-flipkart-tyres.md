# Shared stock and the Flipkart tyre advertising policy

Every eligible Flipkart tyre listing and homogeneous pack draws on the full physical stock of its mapped Tally item, rather than receiving an exclusive allocation, because the seller wants all fulfillable pack sizes to remain purchasable. Calculate complete packs first, then advertise zero for zero complete packs and otherwise `max(5, completePacks)` through a separately named Flipkart tyre policy; the seller accepts rare concurrent-sale exposure until Tally billing is detected and marketplace updates complete, with low-physical-stock highlighting to aid monitoring. This deliberately trades stronger overselling prevention for listing availability, relies on the seller's reported cart-quantity restriction, and must not be generalized automatically to other categories, sellers, or marketplaces.
