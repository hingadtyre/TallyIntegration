# Shared stock and the Flipkart tyre advertising policy

Every eligible Flipkart tyre listing and homogeneous pack draws on the full physical stock of its mapped Tally item, rather than receiving an exclusive allocation, because the seller wants all fulfillable pack sizes to remain purchasable. Calculate complete packs first, then advertise zero for zero complete packs and otherwise `max(5, completePacks)` through a separately named Flipkart tyre policy; the seller accepts rare concurrent-sale exposure until Tally billing is detected and marketplace updates complete, with low-physical-stock highlighting to aid monitoring. This deliberately trades stronger overselling prevention for listing availability, relies on the seller's reported cart-quantity restriction, and must not be generalized automatically to other categories, sellers, or marketplaces.

On 20 November 2026 the seller shortened the exposure period. A verified Flipkart sale reduces available stock across all listings sharing the same Tally item as soon as Stockbridge observes it. The complete-pack and optional advertising-floor rules apply to that available balance. A later matching Tally bill accounts for the same units once.

On 23 November 2026 the seller added a standing keep-back quantity to each Tally stock item. Deduct it once from the shared balance alongside outstanding order reservations. The seller chose to keep the optional tyre minimum after that deduction. For example, eight tyres with two kept back can supply three pairs. A pair listing with the tyre minimum enabled advertises five before any sales allowance. A manual Marketplace stock edit or edited Inventory proposal above the fulfillable pack quantity needs an explicit acknowledgment for that destination when a keep-back applies.
