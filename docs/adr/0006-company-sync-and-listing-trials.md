# Keep automatic stock updates scoped to the Tally company

Several seller accounts can draw on the same Tally stock. Enabling sync grants continuing permission across the company, including mappings that become eligible later. The seller reviews all mapped accounts and locations together before enabling it, even when Inventory is filtered to one account. Pausing an individual listing remains available.

A seller who wants to check a real result first can approve a single calculated quantity at one location while company sync stays off. This gives the seller a verified result without creating a second automatic sync mode. The trial retains existing drafts and stock policies.
