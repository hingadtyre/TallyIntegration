# Flipkart Seller APIs and their fit for Stockbridge

Reviewed on 28 November 2026 for Stockbridge’s local Windows pilot. The review covered the operation pages for all 47 entries in Flipkart’s current public Marketplace reference and all 12 in the separate v2 reference, alongside the seller guides for authentication, order lifecycles, notifications, listings, reports and bulk feeds. The seller-account checks covered order reads only. [Current API index](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/category/mp-api) · [v2 index](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/category/mp-api-2.0.0) · [Seller guide index](https://seller.flipkart.com/api-docs/index.html) · [Production observations](flipkart-validation.md)

## How a sale awaiting pickup affects stock

Flipkart assigns a new order to a seller location and initially reports it as `APPROVED`. The seller can then see it progress through packing to `READY_TO_DISPATCH` while it still awaits pickup. A later dispatch-after time controls when the seller can process it. The goods can already be committed to a verified sale. A shipment can contain several order items and one order item can represent several packs. Count the order-item quantity against the listing's confirmed **Set of**. [Flipkart order lifecycle](https://seller.flipkart.com/api-docs/order-api-docs/OMAPIOverview.html) · [Stockbridge reservation design](../design/architecture.md#matching-flipkart-orders-to-tally-sales-bills)

| Stage in an illustrative sale of two packs of two tyres                  | Tally balance | Kept back | Reserved for this order | Available for marketplace orders |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------: | --------: | ----------------------: | -------------------------------: |
| Before the sale                                                          |            10 |         2 |                       0 |                                8 |
| Stockbridge verifies the seller-fulfilled order while it awaits pickup   |            10 |         2 |                       4 |                                4 |
| A later poll sees the same order packed or ready for pickup              |            10 |         2 |                       4 |                                4 |
| Stockbridge verifies the matching bill and the four-tyre Tally deduction |             6 |         2 |                       0 |                                4 |

The bill can be posted before or after pickup. Pickup by itself never releases the units. If later evidence conflicts, Stockbridge requires review before publishing a positive calculated stock quantity for the affected item. The example gives physical availability in Tally units. The advertised listing quantity also depends on pack size and the seller’s stock policy, including an optional tyre minimum. Company sync and each listing's pause setting govern automatic publication. [Stock and billing rules](../design/architecture.md) · [Shared tyre policy](../adr/0001-shared-stock-flipkart-tyres.md)

Stockbridge checks marketplace orders periodically while running. Five minutes is the default for a new workspace. After the seller confirms starting stock, a covered Standard or Self-ship sale can reserve units across participating sellers in the same Tally company. It needs an identifiable order item, a mapped listing and pickup, a confirmed pack size, and usable Tally and order evidence. Billing-only setup does not activate stock reservations. A new unmapped sale remains visible for mapping and can leave other listings temporarily overstated. A verified pre-pickup cancellation releases the outstanding reservation whoever initiated it. For a billed return, normal sync uses the balance after the seller’s stock entry in Tally. For an unbilled return after pickup, the seller can release the reserved units once the whole order item is back and sellable. [Stockbridge setup](../customer-setup.md) · [Agreed stock rules](../design/architecture.md#matching-flipkart-orders-to-tally-sales-bills)

**Hingad Tyres evidence.** In a 23 November live check an actual Standard order awaiting dispatch qualified as a forward sale even though its courier-return flag was absent. On 28 November all 24 pre-dispatch searches returned zero for the tested Standard and Self-ship orders placed since 25 November. An actual Stockbridge reservation, Tally deduction and marketplace update still need verification on the customer PC. A different order, the shipped CEAT tyre, demonstrated that a successful combined post-dispatch search could omit a real sale. [Live search comparisons and limitations](flipkart-validation.md)

## First delivery and acceptance

**Fix discovery in billing and stock checks together.** The two readers share shipment searches. For post-dispatch, query each of the three supported search states separately, follow every page and deduplicate by seller and order item. Verify the returned profile, shipment type and identity; the seller’s cancellation was also returned by warehouse queries. Keep conflicts visible. Apply the same comparison to a known nonempty pre-dispatch queue before deciding how to group those states. A zero-versus-zero comparison cannot validate a combined filter. [Shipment search contract](https://seller.flipkart.com/api-docs/order-api-docs/v3/PostShipmentSearch.html) · [Observed failures](flipkart-validation.md)

**Prove the full reservation on a mapped sale.** From the normal refresh, discover a seller-confirmed order awaiting pickup without supplying its ID or SKU. Verify its units and the shared available balance, repeat the refresh, observe pickup and then match the Tally bill. Check that the units are counted exactly once, including when billing occurs before pickup. Test a buyer cancellation and a held order separately. The known shipped CEAT should also be discoverable and display its confirmed shipment status even when its bill or stock eligibility still needs review. Show saved evidence and the last successful check when a refresh fails. [Order-matching design](../design/architecture.md#matching-flipkart-orders-to-tally-sales-bills) · [Reservation test cases](../../tests/server/stock-availability.test.ts)

## Where the APIs help this seller

1. **Make missing orders easier to investigate.** The seller can now enter a missing order’s exact ID in Stockbridge and check its shipment, order items, pickup location and state directly. This provides a second source of evidence when a filtered search returns no items. The regular search still has to find orders whose IDs the seller does not already know. Show an item as held, awaiting dispatch, picked up or cancelled using provider evidence, and retain the existing Tally bill and stock-source checks. [Exact order lookup](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/mp-api_versioned/get-shipment-details-by-internal-id) · [Shipment search](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/mp-api_versioned/search-pre-dispatch-shipment-post) · [Local search observations](flipkart-validation.md)
2. **Check slow order reads independently of missing search results.** Flipkart recommends allowing at least 60 seconds for order calls and 10 seconds for listings. Stockbridge now allows 60 seconds per order request. A failed request identifies the affected fulfilment check so the seller can retry it. This addresses premature timeout errors. A successful empty response needs the search comparison above. [Flipkart timeout guidance](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/api-timeout) · [Stockbridge order reader](../../src/adapters/flipkart-billing.ts)
3. **Cross-check the pickup workload.** Flipkart exposes counts by logistics partner for a pickup location. The exact-order result offers a read-only count of shipments awaiting handover at its confirmed location. A count alone cannot identify which order item sold, how many tyres it represents, or whether Tally already deducted it. [Handover counts](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/mp-api_versioned/get-vendor-group-details) · [Order and shipment definitions](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/order-management-intro)
4. **Keep return stock entry in Tally.** For a billed sale, the seller records the units added back to sellable stock in Tally. Normal stock sync picks up that balance. For a shipped sale that was never deducted in Tally, the agreed Stockbridge action releases the outstanding reservation once the seller confirms the goods are back and sellable. Flipkart's return reads may explain an order's history. A separate package-tracking feature remains deferred. [Agreed stock rules](../design/architecture.md#matching-flipkart-orders-to-tally-sales-bills) · [Fetch returns](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/mp-api_versioned/2.0.0/get-returns) · [Stockbridge package research](flipkart-return-package-research.md)

## Capabilities that require another access model or workflow

- **Settlements, expenses and warehouse reconciliation.** Flipkart documents dated sales, settled transactions, financial-year activity, GST, profit and loss, commission invoices and their transaction details. Operational reports cover orders, FBF orders, returns, warehouse stock, stranded or unsellable stock, stock movements and monthly warehouse reconciliation. These could reconcile a payout or explain a fee after sale. Flipkart describes settlement and FBF reports as paid and restricted to third-party partner tokens. The pilot uses seller-owned Self Access. Keep the existing Seller Hub link for a listing’s estimated settlement. The reviewed catalogues identify no per-listing settlement quotation endpoint. [Report types and access](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/reports-api-intro) · [Self Access decision](../adr/0004-seller-owned-flipkart-self-access.md) · [Listing settlement decision](../design/marketplace-listings.md)
- **Faster order and return updates.** Flipkart can notify a receiver when a shipment is created, held, packed, picked up, shipped, delivered or cancelled, when dispatch dates change, and at stages of a return. For a future hosted service, an event could trigger a seller-scoped read while polling catches missed updates and reconciles after downtime. Flipkart requires a reachable HTTPS receiver and subscription setup, including location IDs and security assessment documentation. Delivery can repeat, so applying an event twice must produce the same result. A pilot listening only on the seller’s computer cannot receive Flipkart’s callbacks directly. [Notification service](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/order-management-notifications) · [Subscription requirements and delivery guarantees](https://seller.flipkart.com/api-docs/order-api-docs/NotifIntro.html) · [Event payloads](https://seller.flipkart.com/api-docs/order-api-docs/NotifRef.html)
- **Bulk file feeds.** The separate seller FTP guide describes CSV listing, price and stock feeds for large sellers, especially booksellers. It requires separate provisioning and explicitly excludes multi-location updates. The existing location-specific listing API fits this pilot. [Seller FTP](https://seller.flipkart.com/api-docs/listing-api-docs/LMAPIFtp.html)
- **Account and location onboarding.** Flipkart documents seller registration, location onboarding, seller-owned credentials for own-account use, and partner authorization for acting on behalf of independent sellers. Technical authentication for one seller establishes neither distribution eligibility nor access to paid reports. Keep independent-seller onboarding as a separate product decision.
