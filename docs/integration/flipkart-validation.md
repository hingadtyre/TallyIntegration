## Order evidence for Tally billing review

When fulfilment programmes return the same order item, Stockbridge checks whether their invoice evidence agrees. A verified number from one response and an explicitly missing number or PDF-only result from another leave an invoice-based match under review. A later consistent read can verify it. The stock check also revalidates an invoice number before a bill can release units for publication.

If Flipkart supplies a malformed return-to-origin add-on, the order remains unverified for bill matching and stock reconciliation. A cancellation with this ambiguity cannot release the reservation. Refresh order evidence before relying on the line.

Stockbridge reads order lines using [shipment search](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/mp-api_versioned/search-pre-dispatch-shipment-post) and its [documented pagination](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/mp-api_versioned/search-pre-dispatch-shipment-get). The recent setup preview covers up to 31 days of order dates for the selected account. Search requests explicitly select seller fulfilment (`NON_FBF`) and query normal and self-ship shipments separately before and after dispatch. The reader excludes held, cancelled, return and replacement lines. The full tracked billing check covers additional fulfilment profiles and retains exceptions. A page failure, inconsistent order-item identity or search reaching the provider limit invalidates the review. An empty result only describes the requested scope. See the [seller dispatch and search limits](https://seller.flipkart.com/api-docs/order-api-docs/v3/PostShipmentSearch.html).

The full tracked billing check reads from the company's saved date through a single cutoff. Standard, Self-ship and Express use the `NON_FBF` service profile with shipment types `NORMAL`, `SELF` and `EXPRESS`, respectively. Smart uses `FBF_LITE` and Fulfilment by Flipkart uses `FBF`. Each of those profiles covers all three shipment types, so an express warehouse shipment appears under its warehouse programme. The dispatch service tier alone cannot identify a programme. The reader checks identity against the selected filters and retains held, cancelled and returned order items. An identified order with no confirmed pickup location stays visible for review. [Current Flipkart shipment search contract](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/mp-api_versioned/search-pre-dispatch-shipment-post)

## Apply, policies and recovery

Direct stock edits in Marketplaces require a known quantity and an enabled or disabled pickup location. If the pickup status is unknown, refresh the listing before editing it. This also applies when entering zero at a mapped location because that action can pause Tally sync.

Before an Inventory stock update, the fresh detail response must confirm an active listing and enabled destination. A missing or unrecognized listing status blocks the update even when the previous import showed Active. Flipkart documents the status and archive values in [listing details](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/mp-api_versioned/get-listing-details).

Each Apply saves a durable attempt before sending, checks fresh values and conflicts, preserves the unedited companion price and submits only that edit. It checks per-SKU outcomes and reads back the edited value. A definitive rejection stays failed. For stock, a lost submission reply, warning, ambiguous response or in-progress response leaves the previous request unresolved. An observed quantity alone cannot establish that the old request finished. The exact successful per-SKU result and a fresh listing read serve separate purposes. A recorded completed stock request with inconclusive readback can enter seller-reviewed recovery. The documented inventory response distinguishes a successful update for the individual SKU from failures and warnings. An HTTP-level acknowledgement alone is insufficient. [Flipkart inventory response](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/mp-api_versioned/update-inventory). An unresolved outcome blocks further edits to that listing. In-flight attempts become uncertain after restart and are not automatically replayed.

Imported destinations update Inventory observations without granting a Tally mapping or pack verification. Direct stock Apply pauses the mapped seller listing at the edited location and preserves existing sales allowances. A reviewed manual zero in Inventory also pauses that exact destination. Price edits preserve the destination’s sync setting. Positive live stock under Limited sales is blocked until order reconciliation exists or the seller explicitly returns that destination to Auto. The confirmed policy change retains Pause and sends no stock. Inventory publishes reviewed quantities and recalculates confirmed, eligible destinations after Tally changes. Newly verified orders also trigger decrease checks across eligible listings sharing an item. All Tally calls are reads.

Schema 4 introduced durable destination mapping choices and pending Tally changes. Schema 5 added per-company and per-account billing rules and seller-confirmed order-to-bill links. Schema 6 records the stock-history baseline and observed order obligations. Schema 7 persists pending checks for shared listings and stock request completion evidence. Schema 8 also retains per-company stock-check status. Schema 9 converts manual zero holds into destination pauses during upgrade or restore. Schema 10 retains calculation issues per listing. Schema 11 keeps the catalogue identity of SKU mappings. Schema 12 stores per-item keep-back quantities and schema 13 tracks a pending first calculation after mapping a waiting order. Schema 14 adds the separate billing start date and observed billing orders. Offline restore supports backups from schema 2 through 14. It keeps current billing decisions for configured company/account pairs present in the restored workspace and recovers saved rules and confirmations for other pairs. Conflicting assignments refuse replacement. Restore clears billing and stock order histories and their start dates, pauses listings, disables company automation and marks unfinished updates uncertain. The seller must reconcile and confirm the baseline again before publishing reconciled stock. Credentials stay outside the database.

## Order history for stock publication

Stockbridge reads the seller's selected history start through the time of the check. Each search covers up to 31 days. Adjacent windows share their cutoff and order-item IDs remove duplicates. Each window includes the regular and self-ship variants of the seller-fulfilled NON_FBF service. Pre-dispatch orders are requested separately with hold on and off. Post-dispatch searches request all three documented shipment states, `PICKUP_COMPLETE`, `SHIPPED` and `DELIVERED`. Individual order items can report additional states such as completed, return requested or returned. Stockbridge handles those item states when the provider returns them. Cancellation searches include buyer, seller and marketplace cancellations. The recent billing setup preview uses a single window. The full tracked billing check can span multiple windows. [Shipment search contract](https://flipkart.github.io/fk-api-platform-docs/docs/mp-api_versioned/search-pre-dispatch-shipment-post) and [seller search guide](https://seller.flipkart.com/api-docs/order-api-docs/v3/PostShipmentSearch.html).

The stock read follows each continuation page. At the 5,000-shipment limit it repeats the affected search in shorter windows. Malformed, failed or conflicting reads and a saturated search at a single instant prevent a complete result. A previously seen line that disappears while splitting a search is flagged for review. A held, replacement, returned, courier-return or unfamiliar line retains its identity for review. Only an identified buyer or marketplace forward cancellation with a cancellation timestamp and clear fulfilment flags qualifies as cancelled stock evidence. A previous dispatch or return observation conflicts with an unshipped cancellation and requires reconciliation. A seller cancellation can indicate a shortage. A carrier's return status cannot confirm sellable physical stock. [Flipkart order lifecycle](https://seller.flipkart.com/api-docs/order-api-docs/OMAPIOverview.html).

The supported stock pool covers seller-fulfilled normal and self-ship orders. Sellers must account for older outstanding orders before choosing a start and confirm any stock used by other fulfilment programmes separately. The public search contract gives no historical retention guarantee or atomic cut-off shared with a stock write. An authenticated pilot must compare the covered dates, order identities, held and cancelled lines, return states and totals against Seller Hub before relying on production coverage. Stockbridge has no evidence of orders that the provider silently omitted from every read.
